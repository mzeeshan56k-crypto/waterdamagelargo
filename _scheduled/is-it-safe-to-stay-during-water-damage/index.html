Is It Safe to Stay in Your Home During Water Damage

Published November 18, 2026Water Damage Restoration LargoLargo, FL

Most Largo homeowners can stay in the house during water damage work, and in a contained clean water loss there is usually no reason to leave. The answer changes when the water was contaminated, when the electrical system was involved, or when the affected area includes the only bathroom or kitchen.

The decision comes down to three things: what kind of water it was, which parts of the house are affected, and who lives there.

The Three Water Categories

CategorySourceStaying in the home
Category 1Clean supply line, water heater, rainUsually fine
Category 2Appliance discharge, aquariumUsually fine with the area contained
Category 3Sewage, toilet past the trap, surge or flood waterStay out of the affected area, often stay elsewhere

When Leaving Is the Safer Call

  • Sewage or flood water reached living space
  • The electrical panel or circuits were submerged and power is off
  • A ceiling is sagging and has not yet been taken down
  • The only bathroom or kitchen is inside the work area
  • Someone in the house has asthma, a compromised immune system, or is recovering from surgery
  • Containment for mold removal covers a route you need to use daily

Living Alongside Drying Equipment

Air movers produce steady noise comparable to a strong fan, and the equipment runs continuously, including overnight. Most people adjust within a day.

Dehumidifiers make rooms warmer and drier than usual. Doors into the work area stay closed to keep the drying chamber sealed, which changes how you move around the house.

Ask for placement to be adjusted around bedrooms if sleep is a problem. What cannot be done is switching equipment off at night, because drying is cumulative and shutdowns add days.

Children and Pets

Keep both out of work areas. Equipment has exposed cords and intake grilles, containment sheeting is not a barrier a child should test, and material being removed may have sharp edges.

Cats and small dogs will find their way behind plastic sheeting if there is any gap, so a closed door is more reliable than tape.

What to Ask Before Deciding

  • What category was the water?
  • Which rooms are inside the containment area?
  • Is the electrical system in those rooms safe to energise?
  • How many days will equipment run?
  • Will any demolition produce dust in living space?

Insurance and Additional Living Expense

Many homeowners policies include additional living expense coverage, which pays for temporary accommodation when the home is not reasonably habitable.

That phrase matters. If your crew advises leaving and the reason is documented, that record supports the claim. Ask your adjuster before booking rather than after.

Questions

Staying Home During Water Damage: Common Questions

Do I have to move out during water damage repairs?

Usually not for a contained clean water loss. Sewage, surge water in living space or a submerged electrical system are the cases where staying elsewhere is safer.

Is drying equipment dangerous to be around?

No, but keep children and pets away from cords and intake grilles, and keep the work area doors closed so the drying chamber stays sealed.

Can I sleep in the house with equipment running?

Most people do. Air movers are about as loud as a strong fan, and placement can usually be adjusted around bedrooms.

Can the equipment be turned off at night?

No. Drying is cumulative and every shutdown lets material reabsorb moisture, which extends the job by days.

Is mold remediation safe to stay through?

Usually, with containment and negative air in place. People with asthma, allergies or immune conditions often prefer to stay elsewhere during removal.

What if sewage reached my living room?

Stay out of the affected area. Category three water carries pathogens, and porous material that absorbed it is removed rather than cleaned in place.

Will my insurance pay for a hotel?

Many policies include additional living expense coverage when the home is not reasonably habitable. Ask your adjuster before booking.

Is the house safe once the water is gone?

Not necessarily. Wet electrical components, a sagging ceiling and contaminated material all remain hazards after standing water is removed.

Should I be worried about air quality during drying?

Clean water drying does not usually affect air quality. Where mold is present, containment and HEPA filtration keep the work area separate.

How many days will the equipment be here?

Most residential losses run three to five days. Block walls, tile over slab and hardwood extend that.
