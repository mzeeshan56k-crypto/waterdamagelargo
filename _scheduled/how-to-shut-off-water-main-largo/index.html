How to Find and Shut Off the Water Main in a Largo Home

Published November 12, 2026Water Damage Restoration LargoLargo, FL

The water main shutoff is the most valuable thing to know about your Largo home during a leak. A half inch supply line puts out several hundred gallons an hour, so the minutes between discovering a leak and closing that valve decide most of the damage.

Find it today rather than during an emergency. On most Largo properties it takes under a minute once you know where to look and have the right tool nearby.

The Three Shutoffs Worth Knowing

ValveWhere it isWhat it stops
Fixture stopUnder a sink, behind a toilet, behind the washerThat one fixture
House mainExterior wall where supply enters, or near the water heaterWater to the whole house
Meter valveIn the meter box at the street or kerbWater to the property

Start with the fixture stop if the leak is at a fixture. Go straight to the house main or the meter if water is coming from inside a wall, a ceiling or somewhere you cannot identify.

Where the Meter Box Usually Sits in Largo

Most Largo properties have a rectangular concrete or plastic meter box set flush in the ground near the front property line, often close to the driveway or the kerb.

Lift the lid and you will see the meter with a valve on the street side and sometimes a customer valve on the house side. The customer valve is the one to use.

Many of these valves need a meter key, which is an inexpensive long handled tool from any hardware store. A large adjustable wrench will sometimes work. Keep the key somewhere you can reach in the dark.

Which Way to Turn It

Standard gate valves close clockwise, usually several full turns. Ball valves close with a quarter turn, and the handle sits across the pipe when closed rather than along it.

If a valve will not move, stop. Old gate valves seize and snapping the stem turns one problem into two. Call the utility rather than forcing it.

After the Water Is Off

  • Open a low fixture to drain pressure from the lines
  • Cut power to any wet outlet or circuit if it is safe to reach the breaker
  • Photograph the water line and the failed component before moving anything
  • Lift rugs, boxes and electronics off the floor
  • Call a plumber for the repair and a restoration crew for the water

If the Valve Fails or You Cannot Find It

Call your water provider. In Largo that is the City of Largo for addresses inside the city, and Pinellas County Utilities for many surrounding areas. They can shut service at the main.

Knowing which utility serves your address before an emergency saves a call to the wrong number at the worst moment.

Preparing Before It Happens

  • Locate the meter box and confirm the lid lifts
  • Buy a meter key and keep it near the front door
  • Test the house main once a year so it does not seize
  • Label fixture stops under sinks
  • Make sure everyone in the household knows the location

Questions

Water Shutoff Questions From Largo Homeowners

Where is the main water shutoff in a Largo home?

Usually in a meter box near the front property line, and sometimes on the exterior wall where the supply enters or near the water heater.

What tool do I need to shut off the meter valve?

A meter key, which is an inexpensive long handled tool from any hardware store. Keep one where you can reach it quickly.

Which way do I turn the valve?

Gate valves close clockwise over several turns. Ball valves close with a quarter turn and sit across the pipe when closed.

What if the valve will not turn?

Stop rather than forcing it. Old gate valves seize and snapping the stem makes things worse. Call your water provider to shut service at the main.

Should I shut off the water before calling anyone?

Yes. Every minute of flow adds gallons. Close the valve first, then make calls and take photographs.

Do I need to drain the pipes after shutting off?

Opening a low fixture releases the pressure still in the lines, which stops residual water running out of the failure point.

Who do I call if I cannot stop the water?

Your water provider. City of Largo serves addresses inside the city and Pinellas County Utilities serves many surrounding areas.

Should I turn off the water heater too?

If the water is off and the heater is electric or gas fired, switching it off prevents the element or burner running against an empty tank.

Can I shut off just one fixture?

Yes, using the angle stop under the sink or behind the toilet. Use the main instead when water is coming from inside a wall or ceiling.

How often should I test the shutoff?

Once a year. Valves that are never operated seize in place, which is how owners discover the problem during an emergency.
