How to Tell If Mold Is Growing Behind Your Drywall

Mold behind drywall is common in Largo homes because the back of the board stays wet long after the painted face feels dry. The paper facing on drywall is food, the cavity holds moisture, and nothing in the room shows it until staining or odour appears.

You can usually narrow it down without opening anything. The signs are specific, and the tools used to find the moisture are non destructive.

The Signs That Point Inside the Wall

  • A musty smell strongest at floor level or in a closet
  • A dark or shadowed line along the wall just above the baseboard
  • Paint bubbling, blistering or peeling in a band rather than a patch
  • Drywall that feels soft or gives slightly under thumb pressure
  • Baseboard separating from the wall or cupping
  • Tape seams lifting at the bottom of the wall

A band of damage near the floor is the classic pattern. Water travels sideways across a slab, reaches the wall, then wicks upward through the board, which is why the line sits a few inches to a foot above the floor.

Non Destructive Tools That Find It

A pinless moisture meter reads moisture content through the surface without marking it. Sweeping a wall with one shows where readings rise, which maps the wet area.

Thermal imaging shows surface temperature differences. Evaporating moisture cools a surface, so a wet area typically appears as a cooler region behind the paint. The camera does not see mold, it sees the conditions mold needs.

Used together these two tools usually locate the problem within a small area, which keeps any inspection opening small.

Confirming It Means a Small Opening

Meters and cameras find moisture. Confirming actual growth behind the board requires looking, which means a small inspection cut, usually a few inches, made low on the wall where it is easy to repair.

A borescope through a drilled hole is a smaller alternative when the wall finish is expensive or historic.

What Not to Do

Do not paint over it. Sealing the surface traps moisture in the cavity and the staining returns through the new coat.

Do not spray bleach on the drywall. It adds water to porous material without reaching growth on the back of the board.

Do not open a large area before the source is identified. The demolition is only useful once you know where the water came from.

What Happens If It Is Confirmed

Containment goes up, the moisture source is corrected first, affected porous material is removed and bagged, remaining surfaces are HEPA vacuumed and wiped, and the cavity is dried to a standard before anything is closed back up.

Florida separates mold assessment from remediation, so a project that needs a written protocol starts with an independent assessor.

Mold Behind Drywall: Common Questions

How can I tell if mold is behind my drywall?

Look for a musty smell, a dark band above the baseboard, bubbling paint and board that feels soft. A pinless meter confirms whether the area is wet.

Can mold be found without cutting the wall?

Moisture meters and thermal imaging locate the wet area without damage. Confirming growth on the back of the board still takes a small inspection opening.

Why is the damage always near the floor?

Water spreads sideways across a slab, reaches the wall and wicks upward through the drywall, which leaves a band a few inches to a foot above the floor.

Does thermal imaging see mold?

No. It shows temperature differences caused by evaporating moisture, which identifies where conditions support growth. It is a locating tool, not a diagnosis.

Can I just paint over the stain?

No. Painting seals moisture into the cavity and the staining comes back through the new coat. The moisture source has to be corrected first.

Will bleach fix mold on drywall?

No. Drywall is porous, so bleach adds water near the surface without reaching growth on the back of the board, and the problem often returns.

How much wall has to come out?

Only what the readings require, usually a band cut to a straight line above the wick line. Cutting to the height the moisture reached keeps the rebuild smaller.

Is mold behind drywall dangerous?

It is a health concern mainly for people with asthma, allergies or immune conditions, and it damages material over time. It should be removed rather than sealed in.

How long does mold take to grow in a wall?

In Florida humidity, growth can begin on wet porous material within about twenty four to forty eight hours of the cavity getting wet.

Do I need an independent mold assessor?

For projects needing a written protocol and clearance, yes. Florida generally prevents one company from both assessing and remediating the same job.
