Why Pipes Burst in Florida Homes With No Freeze

Pipes burst in Florida homes all year, and almost none of it has anything to do with cold. In Largo the causes are age, water chemistry, pressure and a handful of components that were never built to last as long as owners assume.

Knowing which part of your plumbing is most likely to fail tells you where to look and what to replace before it floods a room. Most of the failures we see come from a short list.

Supply Hoses Fail More Than Pipes Do

The flexible hose feeding a toilet, sink or washing machine is the single most common failure we attend. Rubber washing machine hoses harden and split. Braided stainless lines corrode at the crimp where the braid meets the fitting.

These parts cost very little and are rated for years, not decades. Braided lines are typically replaced every five to seven years, and rubber hoses sooner.

A failed hose behind a washing machine runs at full pressure until someone finds it. That is why an overnight or holiday failure so often reaches several rooms.

Polybutylene and Early CPVC

Homes built in Largo and the surrounding Pinellas communities during the 1980s and early 1990s often carry grey polybutylene supply pipe with plastic fittings. The material becomes brittle with age and chlorinated water, and failures tend to happen at the fittings.

Early CPVC has a similar story. It performs well for years, then becomes brittle enough that a fitting cracks under normal pressure.

Check under a sink. Grey flexible pipe with plastic fittings usually means polybutylene, and it is worth a conversation with a licensed plumber about a repipe.

Corroded Galvanized Supply Lines

Older Largo homes that were never repiped may still have galvanized steel supply. It corrodes from the inside, which narrows the bore and weakens the wall at the same time.

Warning signs are discoloured water on first draw, dropping pressure at fixtures furthest from the meter, and staining around joints.

Water Heaters on a Bare Slab

A tank sitting directly on a garage or closet slab with no pan is a common arrangement here and a costly one. When the tank lets go it releases forty gallons or more at once, and on a slab that water spreads sideways into the hallway and adjoining rooms.

A drain pan with a routed line turns that event into a wet garage floor. It is inexpensive work and it contains most of the damage.

Pressure and Water Chemistry

High static pressure stresses every fitting in the house. Anything consistently above about eighty pounds per square inch shortens component life, and a pressure reducing valve that has failed open is easy to miss.

What to Check This Month

  • Replace rubber washing machine hoses with braided stainless
  • Close laundry valves when you travel
  • Look under sinks for grey polybutylene pipe
  • Confirm the water heater pan drains outside
  • Test static pressure and the pressure reducing valve
  • Note where your main shutoff is before you need it

Questions

Burst Pipe Questions From Florida Homeowners

Can pipes burst in Florida without freezing weather?

Yes, and most do. Age, water chemistry, pressure and failed flexible connectors cause the great majority of burst pipe losses in Pinellas County.

What is the most common plumbing failure in a Largo home?

A flexible supply hose at a toilet, sink or washing machine. They are inexpensive parts with a service life measured in years rather than decades.

How often should supply lines be replaced?

Braided stainless lines are typically replaced every five to seven years. Rubber washing machine hoses should be replaced sooner or swapped for braided.

How do I know if I have polybutylene pipe?

Look under a sink for grey flexible pipe with plastic fittings. Homes built in the 1980s and early 1990s that were never repiped often still have it.

Does polybutylene affect my insurance?

It can. Some Florida insurers ask about it at renewal and a few decline or surcharge. Ask your agent directly rather than waiting for a claim.

What water pressure is too high?

Anything consistently above about eighty pounds per square inch stresses fittings and shortens the life of hoses and valves throughout the house.

Should my water heater have a drain pan?

Yes, with a line routed to the outside. Without one, a tank failure releases forty gallons or more straight onto the slab and into adjoining rooms.

Can a burst pipe inside a wall be dried without opening it?

Often yes. Air is injected into the cavity through small holes and readings are taken daily. If the cavity will not reach a dry standard, a section comes out.

Is a burst pipe covered by homeowners insurance?

A sudden accidental discharge is generally covered. Long term seepage from a leak that was ignored usually is not, which is why the timeline matters.

What should I do the moment I find a burst pipe?

Shut the water off at the main or the fixture stop, cut power to wet outlets if it is safe, then photograph everything before moving anything.
