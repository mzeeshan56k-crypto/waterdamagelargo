Emergency Response and Water Removal Services

The first phase of any loss: stop the spread, get the water out, and start the structure drying on the first visit.

Emergency Water Extraction

Standing water removal for Largo homes and businesses, measured in gallons per hour instead of buckets. Crews arrive with submersible pumps, truck-mount extractors and weighted carpet wands to clear water off floors before it climbs the drywall. Wet contents get blocked or moved, the water line gets recorded, and drying equipment goes in on the same visit so the structure starts losing moisture immediately.

Emergency Water Extraction details →

Flood Damage Cleanup

Flood damage cleanup for Largo properties that took in groundwater, storm runoff or surge. Crews muck out mud and debris, remove soaked drywall, insulation and flooring to a measured flood cut, then clean and treat the framing before drying starts. Because flood water is treated as contaminated, porous materials come out instead of being dried in place, and the structure is verified dry before rebuild.

Flood Damage Cleanup details →

Structural Drying and Dehumidification

Documented structural drying for Largo properties, with equipment counts calculated from room volume, class of water and affected materials. Low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers handle the load while air movers keep evaporation moving, and specialty tools dry wall cavities, hardwood and cabinet boxes in place. Daily readings prove progress, and equipment stays until the material hits a documented dry standard.

Mold Remediation and Indoor Moisture Services

Florida humidity means mold follows water quickly. These services correct the moisture source, remove affected material and verify the result.

Mold Remediation

Mold remediation for Largo homes where humidity, a slow leak or a past water loss let growth take hold. Work areas are contained under negative pressure with HEPA filtration, affected porous materials are removed and bagged, and remaining surfaces are HEPA vacuumed and wiped. The moisture source is corrected first, because remediation without a dry structure simply grows back.

Mold Remediation details →

Mold Inspection and Testing

Mold inspection and testing for Largo homeowners, buyers and landlords who need facts before spending money. Inspections combine visual assessment, moisture meters, thermal imaging and humidity readings, with air or surface sampling sent to an accredited lab when results will change the plan. You get a written report naming the moisture source, the affected area and the scope of work required.

Mold Inspection and Testing details →

Sewage Cleanup and Sanitizing

Sewage cleanup for Largo homes and businesses hit by a sewer backup, lift station failure or toilet overflow. Crews work the loss as Category 3 water: contaminated liquid extracted and disposed of properly, affected porous materials removed and bagged, hard surfaces cleaned and sanitized, then the structure dried and verified. Containment and HEPA filtration keep the contamination out of clean rooms.

Sewage Cleanup and Sanitizing details →

Storm and Hurricane Damage Repair

Storm and hurricane damage repair for Largo properties dealing with wind-driven rain, roof breaches and window failures. Crews stabilize the opening with tarping or board-up, remove water and wet materials, dry the structure with logged readings, and rebuild the affected interior. Documentation is built for insurance from the first visit, because wind, rain and surge are handled differently on a claim.

Storm and Hurricane Damage Repair details →

Leak, Pipe and Appliance Water Damage Repair

The everyday failures: supply lines, water heaters, roof penetrations and appliances. Fast drying here saves flooring, cabinets and drywall.

Roof Leak Water Damage Repair

Roof leak water damage repair for Largo homes where rain found a way through the roof assembly. Crews relieve trapped water from bulging ceilings, remove wet insulation and drywall, dry the trusses and decking with logged readings, and rebuild the ceiling and paint. The leak path is traced from the attic side so the repair matches where the water actually traveled, not just where it dripped.

Roof Leak Water Damage Repair details →

Burst Pipe and Supply Line Cleanup

Burst pipe and supply line cleanup for Largo homes where pressurized water flooded a room in minutes. Crews extract the water, open and dry the wall cavity around the failure, and dry the flooring assembly before it cups or delaminates. Because supply water is clean, more material can usually be saved, which makes fast drying and accurate moisture mapping worth far more than demolition.

Burst Pipe and Supply Line Cleanup details →

Appliance Leak Cleanup

Appliance leak cleanup for Largo kitchens and laundry rooms, where a supply hose, drain line or ice maker connection has been wetting the floor. Crews read under cabinets and adjacent rooms, dry the cabinet boxes and subfloor in place where possible, and remove only the swollen material that cannot be saved. Slow chronic leaks are treated as potential mold jobs, not just drying jobs.

Ceiling and drywall repair for Largo homes after a water loss, performed only on assemblies that have been dried and documented. Crews replace damaged board, tape and finish, match knockdown, orange peel or smooth textures, seal stains and repaint the plane so the repair disappears. Insulation and any wet framing issue is addressed before the cavity is closed.

Hardwood Floor Drying and Salvage

Hardwood floor drying for Largo homes where a leak reached a wood floor and cupping has started. Specialty mat and panel systems pull moisture up through the boards and out of the subfloor while dehumidification holds the room at a controlled humidity, giving solid wood a real chance to flatten instead of being demolished. Readings guide the process, and we tell you early when a floor will not come back.

Antimicrobial treatment and tack strip inspection are part of the work, so the carpet goes back down clean and flat.

Questions About Our Water Damage Services in Largo

Which water damage service do I need?

Start with what you can see. Standing water needs extraction, a wet structure needs drying, visible growth needs remediation. Most jobs use several in sequence, and we scope it on the call.

Do these services get combined on one job?

Almost always. A typical loss runs extraction, then structural drying, then repair, with mold work added if the water sat long enough. You deal with one crew across all of it.

Which service handles a burst pipe?

Burst pipe cleanup covers extraction and wall cavity drying, and ceiling and drywall repair covers the rebuild. The plumbing repair itself is done by a licensed plumber.

What is the difference between mitigation and restoration?

Mitigation stops the damage getting worse: extraction, drying, removal. Restoration puts the building back: drywall, texture, paint, flooring. Most losses need both.

Can you do mold and water damage work together?

Yes, though Florida rules keep mold assessment and mold remediation separate. We remediate to an independent assessor's protocol when a project needs one.

Are all these services available across Pinellas County?

Yes, in Largo and the surrounding communities. Availability is confirmed when you call, along with an arrival window for your address.

Which services do commercial properties use most?

Commercial water damage restoration, structural drying and sewage cleanup, usually phased around operating hours so the business can stay open.

Do you handle the repair or just the drying?

Both. Crews carry a job from the first extraction pass through drywall, texture, paint and flooring, so there is no gap between mitigation and rebuild.

Which service applies to a roof leak?

Roof leak water damage repair handles the ceiling, attic and insulation. Storm damage repair covers it when wind or a storm caused the opening, including board-up and tarping.

Do I need emergency extraction or just drying?

If there is water standing on the floor, you need extraction first. If the surface is damp with no standing water, drying alone is often enough.

Not Sure Which Service You Need?

Describe what you are seeing on the phone. Source, how long it ran, what flooring is wet. We will tell you which of these services applies and give you an arrival window.
